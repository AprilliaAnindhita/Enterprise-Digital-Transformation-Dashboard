## Enterprise Digital Transformation & Operational Efficiency | Power BI + DAX

### Objective:
The objective of this project is to analyze operational performance, revenue impact, and the role of digital training across global industries using an interactive Power BI executive dashboard.

### Purpose:
The dashboard helps decision-makers and business leaders to:
- Monitor core performance metrics including total industries, average efficiency, and revenue impact.
- Evaluate the specific impact of digital skills training on operational efficiency.
- Compare performance across geographic regions, industry sectors, and company sizes.
- Support data-driven strategies for organizational digital transformation.

### Key Metrics:
- **Total Industries:** 500
- **Avg. Efficiency Impact:** 14.69%
- **Avg. Revenue Impact:** 17.74%
- **Training Impact:** +13.70%

### Insights:
- Active digital skills training drove a +13.70% higher operational efficiency boost compared to companies without training.
- Technology and Retail sectors led in operational efficiency impact (~15.7% and ~15.5%).
- Efficiency impacts were evenly distributed across Small, Medium, and Large company sizes (14% - 15%).
  
### Tools Used:
Power BI Desktop, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions), Data Modeling, and Data Visualization.
